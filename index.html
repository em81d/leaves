Wasatch montane, ~2100 m — model behaviour only, no graphics.

Season environment

Winter precip is the primary driver here: in the western US, a wet Jan–Mar means colour and leaf fall come later. In-season rain only knocks down already-weakened leaves.

Sliders modulate the whole seasonal baseline, not one instant — the sign of a temperature effect depends on where in the season it lands.
