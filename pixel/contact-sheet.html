Bigtooth maple — cell contact sheet, 8 × 6

Rows top → bottom are y 0.82 → 0 (red share → yellow); columns left → right are x 0 → 1 (full green → dead brown). Column 0 is one shared image, because y is undefined on a fully green leaf. ΔE is the alpha-weighted mean colour of each image, averaged in linear light, against the target from grid.js.

Reading it: the two things to check by eye are that each row stays the same hue direction across x, and that each column shades smoothly top to bottom — a cell that jumps is the one that will ghost when Step 3 blends it with its neighbours. ΔE under 2 is invisible side by side, over 5 is clearly off.
